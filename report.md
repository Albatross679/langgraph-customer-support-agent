## Duplicate prevention and restart recovery

- duplicate-before.json records the real API/arq/production LangGraph/Postgres/Redis reproduction. Two conversations proposed ORD-1001 without a business mutation before a decision. Approval changed the order to approved; another thread's rejection overwrote it with rejected. Same-run replay returned 409. Unconditional order mutation caused the bug. Per-run queue deduplication masked cross-thread duplication, and repeated approved-status writes had no visible payout because the refunds were simulated.
- duplicate-after.json records the repaired flow. Approved status remains approved after another thread rejects; identical same-run replay returns 202 without another action. Final regressions also check eight concurrent approvals and ledger-authoritative reconciliation after mutable demo-data edits.
- The fix adds transactional refund_actions and refund_decisions, immutable request decisions, an order-level unique action key, allowed transitions, checkpoint-bound amounts, durable support_runs, Redis/API recovery and Redis AOF/volume persistence. A rejection may be approved through a new review; an approved action is terminal. Recovery does not compete with active thread locks and waits for a bounded orphaned lock after hard crashes with Redis intact. LangGraph, interrupt/resume, arq and the synthetic physical-media store remain in place. No payment provider or real refund was added.
