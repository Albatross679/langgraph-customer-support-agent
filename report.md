- evidence/support-gap-bridge/duplicate-before.json records the real API, arq worker, PostgreSQL checkpointer, Redis and production graph reproduction. Two separate conversations proposed ORD-1001 without an order mutation before a decision. Approval changed it to approved; the second conversation's rejection changed it to rejected. Per-run queue deduplication masked the cross-thread failure, and repeated approved status writes had no visible payout because refunds are simulated.
- duplicate-after.json records the same flow after the initial fix. Approved status remains approved after the second conversation rejects it. Its answer reports an existing approved action, and identical same-run replay returns 202 without another action. Final regressions additionally cover ledger-authoritative reconciliation after mutable employee demo-data edits.
- The fix adds transactional refund_actions and refund_decisions tables, request-key replay checks, serialized allowed order transitions, checkpoint-bound proposal amounts, Postgres-backed support_runs records, immutable decision claiming, worker checkpoint recovery and Redis cache/index repair. Redis has AOF and a persistent volume. Recovery does not compete with active thread locks; a hard crash with Redis intact waits for the bounded 530-second orphaned-lock expiry. Cancellation and Redis loss do not discard recoverable graph state. LangGraph, interrupt/resume, arq and the synthetic physical-media store remain in place. No payment provider or real refund was added.
